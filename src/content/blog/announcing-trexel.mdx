We're extremely happy to announce our upcoming graphics editor - Trexel!

Link: [Trexel.app](https://trexel.app)

Before we dive deeper, I must clarify that **PixiEditor development is unaffected.** We are not abandoning PixiEditor or anything like that. Trexel is just our new thing. 

## Why a new app?

Over the years, we've received many requests for features related to game development in PixiEditor. We love those ideas, and we want to build them into a comprehensive editor for game development.

The nature of PixiEditor is to be a flexible 2D graphics editor. The feature set is broad, and it is use-case agnostic, while Trexel is a specialized app, specifically for game development. It will contain
tools for level editing, texturing, normal maps, materials, skyboxes, game GUIs, and tight game engine integrations.

All the important information is available on [Trexel's website](https://trexel.app).

## What about PixiEditor?

Nothing changes! Its development is unaffected.

In fact, Trexel will help us sustainably fund the development of both PixiEditor and Trexel. This means we can have more full-time employees working on a completely free and open-source PixiEditor, while Trexel is powered by PixiEditor's Core.

So, head out to [Trexel.app](https://trexel.app) and sign up for the newsletter to stay up to date with the latest news.

Cheers!
